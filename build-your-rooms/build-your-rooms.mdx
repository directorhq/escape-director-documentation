Building a Room means setting up what players see and what Game Masters control during a game. Open a Room from **All Rooms → Edit**.

## Recommended order

1. [Configure Live View](/build-your-rooms/configure-live-view): the player screen and end screens.
2. [Add Puzzles and Clues](/build-your-rooms/add-puzzles-and-clues): what players solve and the hints Game Masters send.
3. [Manage Room media](/build-your-rooms/manage-room-media): images, videos, and audio.
4. [Configure Room Automations](/build-your-rooms/configure-room-automations): only if the Room needs actions that run on their own.
5. Connect props and other hardware, if the Room has any. Pick the guide that fits:
   - [Connect Room Devices](/build-your-rooms/connect-room-devices): set up a controller in the app, without code.
   - [Connect existing Devices](/build-your-rooms/connect-existing-devices): props that already send HTTP or MQTT messages.
   - [Build your own controller](/build-your-rooms/build-your-own-controller): a controller running your own code.
6. Open the Room Dashboard on the real Room Station and test a full game.

**Details**, **Live View**, and **Puzzles & clues** save on their own, and **Undo** takes back the last saved change. **Automations** and **Devices** save when you select **Save**.

## You're ready when

- Live View is easy to read on the player screen.
- Every Puzzle and Clue appears in the right order.
- Every image, video, and audio file the Room needs is uploaded.
- Turned-on Automations run their Actions in the right order.
- Connected props report their state and respond in Test mode.
- The success and failure screens show the right message.
