Use these guides to turn a new Room into a tested experience that Game Masters can operate.

## Recommended order

1. [Configure Live View](/build-your-rooms/configure-live-view)
2. [Add Puzzles and Clues](/build-your-rooms/add-puzzles-and-clues)
3. [Manage Room media](/build-your-rooms/manage-room-media)
4. [Configure Room Automations](/build-your-rooms/configure-room-automations) when the Room needs repeatable sequences.
5. [Connect Room Devices](/build-your-rooms/connect-room-devices) when the Room has props or other hardware to connect. For props that already send HTTP or MQTT messages, see [Connect existing Devices](/build-your-rooms/connect-existing-devices). For a controller running your own code, see [Build your own controller](/build-your-rooms/build-your-own-controller).
6. Open the Room Dashboard and test the complete flow on the real Room Station.

Details, Live View, Puzzles and Clues save on their own, and **Undo** takes back the last saved change. Automations and Devices save when you select **Save**.

## You're ready when...

- Live View is readable on the player-facing display.
- Every Puzzle and Clue appears in the intended order.
- Required images, videos, and audio are available.
- Enabled Automations run their Actions in the intended order.
- Connected props report their state and respond in Test mode.
- The success and failure screens show the intended message.
