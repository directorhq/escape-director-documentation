Live View is what players see during a game: the timer, messages, Clue counter, media, and end screens. Set it up in **All Rooms → Edit → Details**. Under **Editing screen**, choose **Live view**, **Success**, or **Failure**.

Changes save on their own. The top of the editor shows **Saved** when they have.

## Configure the normal Live View

With **Live view** selected, work from top to bottom:

1. **Room:** check the name, **Minutes**, and **Clues per game**, and choose the alert tone. To play the tone with every message and Clue, turn on **Play it automatically with every message and clue**. These settings apply to all three screens.
2. **Backdrop & sound:** choose a background color, image, or video. Add an **Intro video brief** or **Soundtrack** if you want one.
3. **Timer**, **Live message**, and **Clue counter:** turn each one on or off and set its look and position. **Live message** also has an optional **Message at game start**. To match your Room's theme, upload a **Clue icon** under **Clue counter**. It replaces the lock icons, and used clues show faded. Remove it to bring the locks back.
4. Watch the **Preview** as you work. To match your Room's screen, type its width and height in the preview header and press Enter. The size saves with the Room. The reset button returns it to 1920 × 1080.

<Warning>
If **Run the clock from this video** is on, the video's length becomes the game length and the countdown is hidden. Play the whole video on the real Room Station before using it with guests.
</Warning>

## Configure the success and failure screens

Choose **Success**, then **Failure**. For each one, check:

- the messages;
- that the text is easy to read over the background;
- whether the timer shows; and
- any media.

## Test Live View on the player screen

The preview shows layout only. Playback, browser permissions, fullscreen, and viewing distance need the real screen.

1. On the Room Dashboard, select **Open Live View**.
2. Stand where players stand and check the screen. Send a test message and play the alert tone.
3. Test both end screens, then reset the Room.
