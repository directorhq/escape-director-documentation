Account Settings holds your profile, sign-in, membership, team, and this
browser's offline state. Open **Account Settings** from the app's side menu or
your account menu.

| Section | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile** | Change the account name, business name, email, or phone number. |
| **Security** | Change your password. |
| **Membership** | Check your plan, manage billing, and see media storage. |
| **Locations** | Add or remove sites. See [Manage Locations](/account-and-help/manage-locations). |
| **Team** | Add Managers and Room Stations. See [Add Managers and Room Stations](/account-and-help/add-managers-and-room-stations). |
| **Beta features** | Try new features early. |
| **This browser** | Check offline access on this browser. |

## Update your profile

In **Profile**, update the account name, business name, or phone number, then
select **Save**. Clear an optional field to remove it.

## Change your email

Your email changes only after you confirm from both your current inbox and the
new one. That way nobody can move your account to another inbox without you.

1. In **Account Settings → Profile**, select **Change** next to **Email**.
2. Enter the new email and select **Send link**.
3. Open the email sent to your current address and select **Confirm change**.
4. Open the email sent to your new address and select **Verify new email**.

Each link works for 1 hour. Until you use the second link, sign in with your
current email. If you signed up with Google, you still sign in with Google.
If you can't open your current inbox, [contact support](/account-and-help/help).

## Review membership and billing

**Membership** shows your status, plan, and current billing period or
cancellation date.

- To change your plan, cancel, or change how you pay, select **Manage billing**.
  It opens Stripe.
- If a payment is needed, select **Update card**.
- **Media storage** shows how much of your storage your Rooms and Leaderboards
  use. Each Location adds 25 GB. See
  [Storage limits](/build-your-rooms/manage-room-media#storage-limits).

<Warning>
Before you confirm a cancellation or plan change, check the date Stripe says it takes effect.
</Warning>

## Change your password

In **Security**, enter your current password and the new one twice, then select
**Change password**. If you've forgotten the current one, use **Forgot
password?** on the sign-in page. Accounts that sign in only with Google or
another linked provider have no password here.

<Danger>
Changing the password signs out every other browser and tablet immediately, including a Room Station signed in as you. Change it between games, not during one.
</Danger>

## Delete your account

Deleting your account removes it and everything in it for good. No one can bring it back, so be sure first.

1. Open **Security** and select **Delete account**.
2. Read the list of what will be deleted: your Rooms with their Puzzles, Clues and Automations, game records and Analytics, Locations, Game Master profiles, Leaderboards, and the number of media files (videos, sounds and images).
3. Enter your password. If you sign in with Google, select **Sign in with Google** first; you come back to the same dialog.
4. Type `DELETE` and select **Delete account**.

When it is done, you land on the Escape Director home page and can no longer sign in. All media in your Rooms, such as videos, sounds and images, is deleted from our storage too. Room Stations clear the room files they saved for offline play within 2 days.

<Danger>
Your membership ends right away and you are not charged again. The rest of the current billing period is not refunded. Every computer signed in to the account is signed out, including Room Stations, so delete the account when no games are running.
</Danger>

Stripe keeps your past invoices and payments for tax records. To stop paying but keep your Rooms and history, cancel in **Membership → Manage billing** instead.

## Try beta features

In **Beta features**, turn a feature on or off with its switch. The change
applies to your whole account. Turning a feature off hides it again, but anything
you made with it stays saved. An empty list means there are no beta features
right now.

## Check this browser

**This browser** shows whether offline access is **Ready**, when it expires, and
which Rooms are downloaded. It covers only this browser profile, not your other
Room Stations.

## Check notifications

Select the bell at the top of the app to open **Notifications**. They include
product updates and a reminder when your free trial ends within 7 days. A dot on
the bell means something is unread. **Mark as read** or **Mark all read** clears
it.

## Sign out

Sign out only after every running game has finished and saved.

Profile, security, and billing changes need an internet connection. Rooms that
are ready offline can still run until their offline access expires.

## If your membership looks wrong

If your membership doesn't match what Stripe shows, or you can't get into your account, [contact support](/account-and-help/help).
