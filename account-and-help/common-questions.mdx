## Rooms, Puzzles, and Clues

### Where do I set up Puzzles and Clues?

In **All Rooms → Edit → Puzzles & clues**. You can also add and edit them from the Room Dashboard. See [Add Puzzles and Clues](/build-your-rooms/add-puzzles-and-clues).

### Can I add or edit a Clue from the Room Dashboard?

Yes. Select the Puzzle, then select **+** at the top of the Clues list. You can add text, image, audio, or video Clues. See [Edit Puzzles and Clues from the Room Dashboard](/build-your-rooms/add-puzzles-and-clues#edit-puzzles-and-clues-from-the-room-dashboard).

### Can I add or edit a Puzzle during a game?

Yes, from the Room Dashboard. A change to **Can be marked complete** applies to the next game.

### Can I upload media from the Room Dashboard?

No. Upload files in **All Rooms → Edit → Media library**. Then you can use them in Clues or send them from the Room Dashboard. See [Manage Room media](/build-your-rooms/manage-room-media).

### Can I send an image, video, or sound that isn't a Clue?

Yes. On the Room Dashboard, use **Images**, **Videos**, or **Audios** under the message box. See [Run a live session](/run-a-session/run-a-live-session).

### What happens if I delete a media file that a Clue uses?

The Clues that use it are deleted too. The confirmation lists them first. Past games stay the same.

### What happens if I delete a Room?

Its Puzzles, Clues, media, Automations, and game history are deleted for good. Its games leave Room Statistics, Analytics, and Leaderboards. See [Delete a Room](/get-started/create-and-configure-your-first-room#delete-a-room).

## Storage

### How much media can I store?

25 GB for each Location on your account, shared by all your Rooms and Leaderboards. Each file can be up to 5 GB. See [Storage limits](/build-your-rooms/manage-room-media#storage-limits).

### Where can I see how much storage I've used?

In **Account Settings → Membership → Media storage**.

### Why did my upload fail?

The file may be over 5 GB, or your storage may be full. Delete media you no longer use, then try again.

## Devices and props

### Which controllers and props work with Escape Director?

There are three ways to connect a prop:

- **Your own firmware:** keep your puzzle logic and add the open-source Device SDK. It includes adapters for the UNO R4 WiFi and GIGA R1 WiFi, and other Arduino boards with USB, Wi-Fi and enough memory can use it through a small board adapter ([board porting guide](https://github.com/directorhq/escape-director-device-sdk/blob/main/BOARD_PORTING.md)). Other platforms can speak the SDK's [MQTT protocol](https://github.com/directorhq/escape-director-device-sdk/blob/main/protocol.md) directly. See [Build your own controller](/build-your-rooms/build-your-own-controller).
- **Escape Director firmware:** set up inputs and outputs in the app without writing code, on an Arduino UNO R4 WiFi or GIGA R1 WiFi. See [Connect Room Devices](/build-your-rooms/connect-room-devices).
- **An existing device:** any device that already sends HTTP or MQTT messages keeps its firmware; you map its messages in Escape Director. See [Connect existing Devices](/build-your-rooms/connect-existing-devices).

## Running games

### Can a Room run without internet?

Yes, once the Room is ready offline in that browser. Its card in **All Rooms** shows **Offline ready until** a date and time, and **Account Settings → This browser** lists the downloaded Rooms. See [Prepare your Room Station](/prepare-for-a-shift/prepare-your-room-station).

### Can I edit Puzzles or Clues while offline?

No. Editing needs a connection.

## Locations and team

### How do I add another site?

Open **Account Settings → Locations** and select **Add Location**. Each extra Location adds to your membership price. See [Manage Locations](/account-and-help/manage-locations).

### Can my staff have their own login?

Managers can, at no extra cost. Game Masters pick their name from a list on the Room Dashboard instead of signing in. See [Add Managers and Room Stations](/account-and-help/add-managers-and-room-stations) and [Manage Game Masters](/prepare-for-a-shift/manage-game-masters).

### Can the computer that runs a Room sign in without my password?

Yes. Set it up as a Room Station with a short code. See [Set up a Room Station](/account-and-help/add-managers-and-room-stations#set-up-a-room-station).

## Account

### How do I change or cancel my plan?

Open **Account Settings → Membership** and select **Manage billing**. See [Use Account Settings](/account-and-help/use-account-settings).

### How do I delete my account?

Open **Account Settings → Security** and select **Delete account**. It deletes your Rooms, game history and the rest of your account data for good (Stripe keeps past invoices), and ends your membership right away without a refund. See [Delete your account](/account-and-help/use-account-settings#delete-your-account).

### How do I change my email address?

Contact support. See [Troubleshoot and get help](/account-and-help/help).

### Can I try new features early?

Yes. Open **Account Settings → Beta features** and turn on the ones you want. See [Try beta features](/account-and-help/use-account-settings#try-beta-features).

### What does the dot on the bell mean?

You have an unread notification, such as a product update or a trial reminder. See [Check notifications](/account-and-help/use-account-settings#check-notifications).
