---
title: "Account"
description: Manage your profile, sign-in security, membership, billing, team, and this browser's offline state.
---

Use [Account Settings](/account-and-help/use-account-settings) to update your profile, review sign-in security, manage membership, billing and media storage, try beta features, check notifications, check this browser's offline state, sign out, or delete your account.

Use [Manage Locations](/account-and-help/manage-locations) to add a site, move Rooms between sites, or remove one.

Use [Add Managers and Room Stations](/account-and-help/add-managers-and-room-stations) to give people their own login, or let the computers that run your Rooms sign in on their own.

For account access or billing help, [contact support](/account-and-help/help).
