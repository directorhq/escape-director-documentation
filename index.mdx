Escape Director runs your escape rooms from a web browser. You build each Room's Puzzles, Clues, and player screen, run live games from the Room Dashboard, and review results in Room Statistics, Analytics, and Leaderboards.

## Choose your path

### If you're an owner setting up Escape Director

1. [Create your account and sign in](/get-started/create-your-account-and-sign-in).
2. [Activate your plan](/get-started/start-your-trial-and-activate-your-plan).
3. [Create your first Room](/get-started/create-and-configure-your-first-room).
4. [Build your Room](/build-your-rooms/build-your-rooms).
5. [Prepare the Room Station](/prepare-for-a-shift/prepare-your-room-station).

### If you're a Game Master preparing for a shift

1. [Prepare the Room Station](/prepare-for-a-shift/prepare-your-room-station).
2. Keep the [Live session quick reference](/run-a-session/live-session-quick-reference) nearby.
3. [Run a live Room Session](/run-a-session/run-a-live-session).
4. If something looks wrong, see [Troubleshoot a live session](/run-a-session/troubleshoot-a-live-session).
