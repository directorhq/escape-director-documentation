# Use Analytics

Use Analytics to see how your Rooms play across completed, non-excluded Room Sessions.

## Choose what to look at

1. Select **Analytics**.
2. At the top of the page, choose **One room**, **Compare rooms**, or **Compare Game Masters**.
3. Choose the Room, or the Rooms or Game Masters to compare, and a period such as **Last 90 days** or **All time**.
4. Read each card's figures together with its footer, which says what was counted and how many games it is based on.

**Compare rooms** shows two to five Rooms side by side. **Compare Game Masters** shows two to five Game Masters within one Room. Everyone on the account can see Game Master comparisons.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/analytics-compare-game-masters.png" alt="Compare Game Masters view for The Alchemist's Study, with a Game Masters side by side table listing games, escapes, clues per game and average time left for five Game Masters"><figcaption><p>Compare Game Masters keeps one Room and adds a row for each Game Master.</p></figcaption></figure>

Cards show the numbers without judging them. A Room or Game Master with fewer than 10 games is marked in comparison tables rather than hidden.

## Read the cards

The page starts with these cards:

- **Room at a glance**: games, escape rate, clues, and time left.
- **Escape rate and clues over time**: one point per week, or per month over 12 months or more. Weeks without completed games are left as gaps.
- **When groups need help**: the share of games with a clue or message at each point of the game, with lines for when each Puzzle is usually solved.
- **How close escapes are**: time left on escapes, in 2-minute steps.
- **Group size**: escapes and clues by number of players.
- **Puzzle by puzzle**: clues for each Puzzle and when it is usually solved.

When groups need help and Puzzle by puzzle show one Room at a time, because each Room has its own Puzzles and length. When you are comparing Rooms, select **Show one room** on these cards.

Older games that were saved before Escape Director recorded game clock times use estimated times on When groups need help. The card's footer says how many games are estimated.

## Add, arrange, and remove cards

- Select **Add card** to see a preview of every card and add one. Extra cards include **Each clue**, **How the room opens**, **Pauses and time added**, **First-timers and experienced players**, **Rooms side by side**, and **Game Masters side by side**.
- Open a card's menu and choose **Move up** or **Move down** to change its place.
- Choose **Remove from dashboard** to remove a card. Select **Undo** if you removed the wrong one.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/analytics-add-card.png" alt="Add a card window showing a preview of every card, grouped under How the room plays, Where groups need help, Who is playing, and Rooms and Game Masters"><figcaption><p>Add card shows a preview of each card and marks the ones already on the page.</p></figcaption></figure>

## Change one card's settings

Open a card's menu to:

- choose **Rooms, Game Masters and period** to give that card its own Rooms, Game Masters, or period instead of the page's;
- choose **About this card** to see exactly what the card counts; or
- choose **Download CSV** to download the card's figures.

## Set an escape-rate target

Room at a glance, Escape rate and clues over time, and Rooms side by side can show a target beside the escape rate.

1. Open the card's menu and choose **Escape-rate target**.
2. Enter a whole number from 1 to 100 for each Room.
3. Select **Save**.

Each Room has one target, shared by everyone on the account.

## Ask Analytics

Select **Ask Analytics** to ask about your Rooms in plain language. Start with a suggested review or type your own question.

Ask Analytics can split a figure by day of the week, weekday or weekend, time of day, or Game Master, and follow it week by week or month by month. For example, you can ask which days a Game Master who often adds time usually works. It shows the numbers behind each answer. When your data can't answer a question, it says so instead of guessing.

## You're ready when...

You can say what a card shows, which games it counts, and how many games it is based on.
