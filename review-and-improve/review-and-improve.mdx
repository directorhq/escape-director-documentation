description: "Review saved Room Sessions in Room Statistics, find trends in Analytics, and manage public Leaderboards."
---

Every saved Room Session feeds three places. Pick the one that matches your question.

| To… | Open |
| --- | --- |
| Look at one game: its notes, Session Log, and result | [Room Statistics](/review-and-improve/view-room-statistics) |
| See patterns across many games | [Analytics](/review-and-improve/use-analytics) |
| See rankings or set up the public display | [Leaderboards](/review-and-improve/manage-leaderboards) |

<Info>
These views use what was saved at the end of each Room Session. The data is more useful when every game names the right Game Master and has its details filled in.
</Info>
