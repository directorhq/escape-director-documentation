Getting started takes three steps. When you finish, you have an active membership and a Room ready to build.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your email address or Google account. See [Create your account and sign in](/get-started/create-your-account-and-sign-in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your membership">
    Choose a plan and finish Stripe checkout. See [Start your trial and activate your plan](/get-started/start-your-trial-and-activate-your-plan).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first Room">
    Name the Room and set its starting time and Clue count. See [Create your first Room](/get-started/create-and-configure-your-first-room).
  </Step>
</Steps>
