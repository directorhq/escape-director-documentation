---
title: "Start your trial and activate your plan"
description: "Choose an Escape Director plan, finish Stripe checkout, and check that your membership is active."
---

After you sign in for the first time, Escape Director asks you to choose a plan. Payment happens on a secure Stripe checkout page.

## Start the trial

1. Follow the membership prompt, review the plan, and continue to Stripe checkout.
2. Enter your billing details, complete checkout, and return to Escape Director.

<Info>
Stripe handles the payment form. Escape Director never asks for payment details by chat or email.
</Info>

## Check that your membership is active

Open **Account Settings → Membership**. **Status** should be active, with the plan you chose under **Plan**.

If the app still shows the membership prompt, sign out and sign back in once.

## If checkout doesn't finish

- Check the billing details Stripe shows.
- If the checkout page expired, return to the membership prompt and start checkout again.
- After a successful payment, don't submit the form again.
- If Stripe confirms payment but Escape Director doesn't show an active membership, [contact support](/account-and-help/help).
