---
title: "Create your account and sign in"
description: Sign up for Escape Director with email or Google, verify your email address, and sign in.
---

Sign up from [www.escapedirector.com](https://www.escapedirector.com/). After you sign in, Escape Director opens **All Rooms**.

## Before you begin

Have an email inbox you can open now and a password you can store safely. Or use a Google account.

## Create the account

1. Go to [www.escapedirector.com](https://www.escapedirector.com/) and select **Start free trial**.
2. Enter your name, email address, and password. Your business name is optional.
3. Submit the form.

To use Google instead, select **Continue with Google** and choose the account. Google accounts don't need email verification, so skip the next section. Sign in later with **Continue with Google**.

## Verify your email address

1. Open the verification email from Escape Director and select its link. If you asked for more than one, use the newest.
2. Return to Escape Director and sign in with the same email address.

## If you can't sign in

- Check that you verified your email address.
- Look in your spam or junk folder for the newest verification email.
- If your password isn't accepted, select **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page.
- If you still can't get in, [contact support](/account-and-help/help).
