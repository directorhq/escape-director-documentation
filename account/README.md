# Account

Use [Account Settings](use-account-settings.md) to update your profile, review sign-in security, manage membership, billing and media storage, try beta features, check notifications, check this browser's offline state, sign out, or delete your account.

Use [Manage Locations](manage-locations.md) to add a site, move Rooms between sites, or remove one.

Use [Add Managers and Room Stations](add-managers-and-room-stations.md) to give people their own login, or let the computers that run your Rooms sign in on their own.

For account access or billing help, [contact support](../help/README.md).
