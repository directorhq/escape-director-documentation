---
title: "Prepare for a shift"
description: "Before guests arrive, update the Game Master list, prepare the Room Station, and check Live View."
---

Do these checks before the first group enters a Room.

1. If staff changed, [manage Game Masters](/prepare-for-a-shift/manage-game-masters).
2. On the computer that will run the Room, [prepare your Room Station](/prepare-for-a-shift/prepare-your-room-station).
3. Open Live View on the player screen and check that the Room is in its starting state.
